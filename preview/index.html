How we work

Five stages. No surprises in stage four.

Every project follows the same sequence, and you see the plan and the cost before we build anything.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We read your org, interview the people who use it, and rank what is actually hurting.

    1 to 2 weeks
  2. 02

    Blueprint

    Data model, automation map, integration plan and a fixed quote. You approve before we touch production.

    1 week
  3. 03

    Build

    Two-week sprints in a sandbox, with a demo at the end of each one. Nothing lands unseen.

    2 to 10 sprints
  4. 04

    Launch

    Data migration rehearsal, cutover plan, training, and hypercare while your team gets comfortable.

    2 weeks
  5. 05

    Sustain

    Optional managed hours, release reviews and a quarterly roadmap so the org keeps pace.

    Ongoing

About Anduril

Named after a sword that was broken, then reforged.

Anduril is a founder-led Salesforce consultancy. We are small on purpose: the consultant who scopes your project is the one who builds it, and the one you can call after launch. No hand-off to a bench, no junior team behind a senior sales call.

We say no to work you don't need.

If a standard feature does the job, we configure it. Custom code is the last resort, not the first quote.

We leave your admin stronger.

Every build comes with documentation and a walkthrough. The goal is an org your team can run without us.

We measure adoption, not delivery.
