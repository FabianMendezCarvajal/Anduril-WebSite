Most companies' systems were never designed. They were settled, one tool, one spreadsheet and one integration at a time, by people who have since moved on. We are the people you call when nobody can draw the map any more: we chart it before we change it.

Andúril is the name of the reforged sword in Tolkien's Middle-earth, "Flame of the West." Our compass rose points there: one long point, lit in gilt, the only flame the mark carries.

02Terrain

Three signs the ground has shifted.

Reports nobody trusts

Revenue changes depending on which system you ask and who pulls the report. Leadership keeps a spreadsheet "just in case".

Systems that disagree

CRM, ERP, billing and support each keep their own version of the customer. People copy data between them by hand.

A roadmap that never starts

Every new request is a fire. The transformation stays on a slide because the backlog never ends.

04The method

Five bearings, six terrains.

The Westward Method is how every engagement runs: the same five bearings, each ending at a waypoint you sign before we move on. The terrain changes with the route. The method does not. Choose a route.

  1. 01SurveyWalk the ground

    We read everything before we touch anything.

  2. 02ChartDraw the map

    Every change gets a cost and a reason. You approve the price before we build.

  3. 03ForgeCut the route

    Two-week sprints, a demo at the end of each, nothing in production unseen.

  4. 04LaunchCross over

    A rehearsed cutover, training by role, and a consultant on call.

  5. 05KeepKeep the map current

    Each review becomes the next survey. The route loops.

Waypoint: a decision you sign. Nothing crosses to the next bearing without it.Sheet: what you keep from each bearing, whether or not you go on with us.
