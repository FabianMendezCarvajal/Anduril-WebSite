Most orgs were never designed. They were settled, one field and one workflow at a time, by admins who have since moved on. We are the people you call when nobody can draw the map any more: we chart it before we change it.

Andúril is the name of the reforged sword in Tolkien's Middle-earth, "Flame of the West." Our compass rose points there: one long point, lit in gilt, the only flame the mark carries.

02Terrain

Three signs the ground has shifted.

Reports nobody trusts

Pipeline numbers change depending on who pulls them. Leadership keeps a spreadsheet "just in case".

Automation that fights itself

Workflow Rules, Process Builders and Flows stacked over years. Nobody dares to switch one off.

An admin buried in tickets

Every new request is a fire. Roadmap work never starts because the backlog never ends.

03Routes

Six routes through the backlog.

Fixed-scope where it can be, retained where it should be. Each service carries its legend symbol; open a row.

  • Sales, Service and Experience Cloud from a blank org. We design the object model around how your team sells and serves today, not around a template, and migrate what is worth keeping from HubSpot, Dynamics, Zoho or spreadsheets.

    • Discovery workshops with sales, service and finance
    • Object model, layouts, permission sets, record types
    • Migration rehearsal before cutover, 30-day hypercare
    • 6 to 14 weeks, fixed price after the Chart stage

  • Several admins, no single owner, and nobody can explain why a record does what it does. We survey everything, rank the findings by business impact, and pay down the debt in sprints without stopping the business.

    • Technical survey with a prioritised, costed fix list
    • Workflow Rule and Process Builder migration to Flow
    • Field, profile and report clean-up with usage evidence
    • 2-week survey, then 2 to 6 sprints

  • Salesforce has to agree with your ERP, your billing tool and your support desk. We build the integrations and the hygiene rules that keep them agreeing.

    • ERP, billing, marketing and support integrations
    • MuleSoft, REST, platform events, Make or Workato
    • Matching rules, deduplication, one-time merge
    • 3 to 8 weeks per integration

  • Some processes clicks alone can't handle. We write the code, we write the tests, and we make sure the next developer can read both.

    • Apex, Lightning Web Components, complex Flows
    • Agentforce agents, prompt templates, guardrails
    • Test coverage above 85%, version control, CI
    • Scoped per feature, 2-week sprints

  • The requests keep coming. A named consultant handles them within an agreed SLA and keeps the org ready for every Salesforce release.

    • Monthly block of hours with a named consultant
    • Release readiness review, three times a year
    • Quarterly roadmap and health report
    • Retainer from 20 hours a month

  • The org is built well, but people work around it. We train by role, coach your admin, and measure what sticks.

    • Role-based sessions for reps, agents and managers
    • Admin coaching so your team owns the org
    • Adoption dashboards: logins, data entry, report views
    • 2 to 4 weeks
04The survey

Five stages. No surprises in stage four.

You see the chart and the fixed cost before we touch production, and a working demo at the end of every sprint.

090 days · a typical rescue
01

Survey

Walk the ground1 to 2 weeks

We read your org the way a surveyor reads a valley: every object, every automation, every report opened in the last 90 days. Then we interview the people who use it.

You get: an org survey ranked by business impact.

02

Chart

Draw the map1 week

Findings become a design: data model changes, an automation map, an integration plan and a release sequence. Every item has a cost and a reason.

You get: a fixed-price quote you approve before we build.

03

Build

Cut the route2 to 10 sprints

Two-week sprints in a sandbox. Each ends with a demo you and your admin attend, and a written change log. Nothing lands in production unseen.

You get: working features every two weeks, documented as we go.

04

Launch

Cross over2 weeks

Migration rehearsed before the real cutover. Training by role, then hypercare while the first real reports run.

You get: a cutover plan, recordings, and a consultant on call.

05

Keep

Keep the map currentOngoing, optional

Managed hours, a review before each Salesforce release, and a quarterly roadmap so the org keeps pace with the business instead of drifting again.

You get: a named consultant, an SLA, and a health score that moves.

08The map-maker

Founder-led, and small on purpose.

The consultant who surveys your org is the one who builds the route, and the one you can call after launch. No hand-off to a bench, no junior team behind a senior sales call.

The rose · Andúril, "Flame of the West"
A compass that points west.

Eight points: four cardinal, four between. One is longer than the others and lit in gilt. The instrument of anyone who has to find their way through unfamiliar ground.

  • We say no to work you don't need.

    If a standard feature does the job, we configure it. Custom code is the last resort, not the first quote.

  • We leave your admin stronger.

    Every build ships with documentation and a walkthrough. The goal is an org your team can run without us.

  • We measure adoption, not delivery.

    A feature nobody uses is a failed feature. We track logins, data entry and report views after launch.

  • Certified, and still hands-on.

    Salesforce-certified consultants who still build in the sandbox every week.
