01

Assess

1 to 2 weeks

We read your org the way an auditor reads accounts: every object, every automation, every report opened in the last 90 days. Then we interview the people who use it.

You get: an org health report ranked by business impact.

02

Blueprint

1 week

Findings become a design: data model changes, an automation map, an integration plan and a release sequence. Every item has a cost and a reason.

You get: a fixed-price quote you approve before we build.

03

Build

2 to 10 sprints

Two-week sprints in a sandbox. Each ends with a demo you and your admin attend, and a written change log. Nothing lands in production unseen.

You get: working features every two weeks, documented as we go.

04

Launch

2 weeks

Migration rehearsed before the real cutover. Training by role, then hypercare while the first real reports run.

You get: a cutover plan, recordings, and a consultant on call.

05

Sustain

Ongoing, optional

Managed hours, a review before each Salesforce release, and a quarterly roadmap so the org keeps pace with the business instead of drifting again.

You get: a named consultant, an SLA, and a health score that moves.
