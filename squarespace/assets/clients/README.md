# Client logos

The white files are embedded in `code-blocks/clients-grid.html` and the preview's sheet 06; the full-colour files are kept for
anywhere a light background suits. All are trademarks of their owners; show them only with each client's permission, and
don't crop or redraw them.

| File | Mark | Where it came from |
|---|---|---|
| `fifa-world-cup-26.webp` / `.png` | The FIFA World Cup 26 master emblem (unveiled May 2023): the trophy in front of a black "26", FIFA in the 6 | Rendered at 311 × 480 from the vector copy in the npm package `football-badges` 0.1.3 (`badges/competitions/fifa-world-cup-2026.svg`). Its geometry matches a second, independently sourced copy to within antialiasing. Not downloaded from FIFA: ask your FIFA contact for the approved artwork and replace these files if it differs. |
| `colsubsidio.svg` | Colsubsidio's current logo (brand evolution of July 2021): the yellow tangram arrow and the blue rounded wordmark, `#FFD000` and `#0067B1` | A byte-level copy of the header logo on colsubsidio.com (`/assets/icons/logo-colsubsidio.svg`), taken from two independent public copies that match exactly. Scripts and external references were stripped; there were none. |
| `fifa-world-cup-26-white.svg` | The one-colour reverse of the emblem: white "26", the trophy and FIFA cut out so the background shows through | Vector from a public GitHub copy (akdevv), with its dark panel removed and the viewBox tightened. Its "26" and FIFA match the master emblem to within 0.7% of pixels; its trophy cut-out is a smoothed trace of the master trophy (about 94% overlap). Not from FIFA: replace with FIFA's own one-colour artwork if your contact supplies it. |
| `colsubsidio-white.svg` | Colsubsidio's current logo in one-colour white | `colsubsidio.svg` with every fill set to white; the shapes are unchanged. |
