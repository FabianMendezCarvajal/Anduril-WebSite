## The method (sheet 04)

*Coordinates:* 04 / The method

*H2:* Five bearings, *six terrains.*

*Lede:* The Westward Method is how every engagement runs: the same five bearings, each ending at a waypoint you sign before we move on. The terrain changes with the route. The method does not. Choose a route.

*Code Block:* `code-blocks/method-routes.html`. The five bearings in a row (Survey · Walk the ground, Chart · Draw the map, Forge · Cut the route, Launch · Cross over, Keep · Keep the map current), the key to waypoints and sheets, then six route tabs. Each route shows its question, a scale bar with the time to its first result, five legs (each with its Sheet, Time and Waypoint), what we measure, and a *Start this route* button to `/contact`. The tabs are CSS only and work on every plan.
