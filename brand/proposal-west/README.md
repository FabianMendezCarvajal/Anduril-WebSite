# Proposal B · The West

A second, unrelated identity for Anduril, built on a different metaphor from the forge: **the survey**. A
consultancy that inherits a Salesforce org inherits territory nobody has charted; we map it before we touch it.
The identity is that map: ivory paper, pine ink, contour lines, coordinates, and a compass rose whose west
point is lit in gilt, because Andúril means "Flame of the West".

```
proposal-west/

  logo/mark/       the West Rose: ink on ivory, ivory on pine, gilt, mono
```

## The mark

An eight-point compass rose. The west point is longer than the rest and filled Gilt; it is the only accent the
mark carries. Reads at 16 px. The seal (rose, double ring, name above, promise below) is for stickers, stamps
and card backs, never the everyday logo.

## Colour

Ivory `#F4EFE4` is the ground, Pine `#143D31` the ink and the occasional dark ground, Gilt `#C9A14B` the flame:
one point, one word or one line per surface. Gilt is never body text; Gilt Deep `#7A5D18` is its text form on
Ivory (AA). Full palette with RGB, CMYK and contrast in `colors.json`.

## Type

* **Fraunces** 500, optical size 144, SOFT 30, WONK 1: headlines and the wordmark. Italic 400 in Gilt Deep for
  the turn of phrase ("Salesforce, *mapped.*").
* **Instrument Sans** 400/500: body and UI.
* **DM Mono** 400/500: coordinates, labels, captions, map margins.

## Graphics

Contour lines (generated, `graphics/contours-moss.svg`), map-sheet margins with ticks and coordinates, a legend
block for services and findings, a scale bar for timelines, and the seven stars as a small chart. Every surface
is treated as a sheet of the survey.

## Voice

"Salesforce, mapped." Survey, sheet, field notes, route, legend: the vocabulary of the map, applied to the org.
